Try Our Virtual Lab

Experience our interactive biology simulations with this free demo of our DNA extraction experiment. Perfect for students and educators alike.

DNA Extraction Demo

Follow these steps to extract DNA from a strawberry sample in our virtual lab environment:

  1. Add strawberry sample to the beaker
  2. Pour in extraction buffer and mix gently
  3. Filter the mixture through cheesecloth
  4. Precipitate the DNA with cold alcohol
  5. Observe and collect the DNA strands
