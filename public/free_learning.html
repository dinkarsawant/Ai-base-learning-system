Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform

Is there really no cost for the Basic plan?

Yes! Our Basic plan is completely free forever. You'll have access to dozens of high-quality courses and basic features without ever needing to pay. We believe education should be accessible to everyone.

What's included in the free trial for Pro Learner?

The free trial gives you full access to all Pro features for 7 days, including all courses, advanced analytics, and premium puzzle games. No credit card is required to start the trial. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends with no charge.

How does the student verification work?

After signing up for the Student plan, you'll be asked to provide proof of current student status (like a student ID or enrollment verification). Our team typically verifies documents within 24 hours. The discount is valid as long as you remain a student.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately for upgrades or at the end of your billing cycle for downgrades.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and in some regions, Apple Pay and Google Pay. All payments are processed securely through our PCI-compliant payment processor.
